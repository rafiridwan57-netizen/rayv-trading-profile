About My Trading

I started learning trading in 2022, beginning with the fundamentals of forex and chart analysis. From there, I expanded my knowledge by studying various technical analysis concepts.

Over time, my focus shifted primarily to XAU/USD, where I began developing my scalping and intraday/day trading styles.

I have no desire to rely on signals from others. Instead, I'm committed to understanding the market, building my own strategies, controlling risk, and becoming a consistent trader.

My goal: Discipline → Consistency → Growth

My Trading Style

Scalping

I use scalping to find setups with clear confirmation and relatively quick entry. Quick exits with defined risk.

Intraday

I enjoy trades that last around 1-2 hours when the market provides clear setups. Capturing intraday momentum and structure breaks.

Day Trading

I prefer following trends and waiting for retracement/retest rather than chasing price. Patience-based approach to entries.

"I don't need to trade every move. I only need to trade the moves that make sense."

Technical Concepts

Support & Resistance

Key price levels where buyers and sellers congregate, affecting price reversals and bounces.

Supply & Demand

Zones where imbalances create momentum and reversals based on volume and price interaction.

Order Block

Areas where institutional orders accumulate, creating significant price reactions and structure.

Smart Money Concepts

Understanding institutional behavior, liquidity sweeps, and smart money positioning.

Market Structure

Analysis of higher highs/lows, breakouts, and structural changes in price movement.

BOS (Break of Structure)

When price breaks established highs or lows, signaling potential trend changes.

CHoCH (Change of Character)

Shift in market character from trending to ranging or vice versa.

Liquidity

Identifying areas where liquidity pools are located for potential reversals.

Trendline

Visual representation of trend direction and support/resistance in trending markets.

Price Action

Reading price movement and candlestick formations without indicators.

Wick Rejection

When wicks reject key levels, signaling buyer or seller strength.

Breakout

Price breaking out of consolidation or key resistance/support levels.

Break & Retest

Price breaking a level and retesting it, creating entry opportunities.

EMA 50

50-period moving average for identifying medium-term trend direction.

EMA 200

200-period moving average for identifying long-term trend bias.

Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Analyzing multiple timeframes to confirm bias and find confluences.
